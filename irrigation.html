Why Irrigation is Delayed / ಕಾರಣ:

Imminent precipitation (4.2 - 8.5 mm) is forecasted for Hassan within the next 24 to 48 hours. Soil moisture currently stands at 45%, which is optimal for the tillering stage of Finger Millet. Applying canal or borewell irrigation now would risk waterlogging, leach soil nitrogen, and unnecessarily consume electricity.

Farmer Action Plan / ರೈತರು ಕೈಗೊಳ್ಳಬೇಕಾದ ಕ್ರಮಗಳು:
1. Hold Off Pumping / ಪಂಪ್ ಚಾಲೂ ಮಾಡಬೇಡಿ

Do not operate borewell or drip lines for 48 hours.

ಮಳೆ ಮುನ್ಸೂಚನೆ ಇರುವುದರಿಂದ ಎರಡು ದಿನ ನೀರು ಹಾಯಿಸಬೇಡಿ.

2. Clear Drainage / ಬಸಿಗಾಲುವೆ ಸ್ವಚ್ಛಗೊಳಿಸಿ

Ensure excess rainwater flows easily out of field corners.

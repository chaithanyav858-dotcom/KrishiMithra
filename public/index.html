Informed Decisions

Avoid traditional guesswork. Choose crops matched to your soil's actual retention ability, regional climate, and market cycle.

Timely Action

Spray pest treatments only when winds are gentle and rain won't wash away chemicals. Prevent seed loss from sudden downpours.

Water Awareness

Conserve borewell water and diesel electricity by stopping unnecessary irrigation when rainfall is forecasted within 24 hours.
